Visa Requirements

Offer letter, Irish D visa, proof of funds (EUR 7,000), insurance, and GNIB registration on arrival with complete supporting records.

Work Rights

20 hours/week during term, 40 hours in summer, and 24-month stay-back permission for eligible graduates.
