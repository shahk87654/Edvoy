Visa Requirements

  1. Receive I-20 form from your US university
  2. Pay SEVIS fee (USD 350)
  3. Complete DS-160 visa application
  4. Schedule F-1 interview at US Embassy Islamabad
  5. Attend interview with full documents
  6. Receive visa and prepare departure

Required documents: passport, I-20, DS-160, SEVIS receipt, proof of funds, acceptance letter, transcripts, test scores, interview confirmation.

Work Rights

During study: up to 20 hours/week on-campus
During breaks: full-time on-campus
OPT: 12 months post-study
