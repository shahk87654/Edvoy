Visa Requirements

Acceptance letter, VLS-TS application, Campus France process, financial proof (EUR 615/month), accommodation, and online visa validation on arrival.

Work Rights

Up to 964 hours/year during study, 12-month APS job search route, and extended stay options after qualifying employment.
